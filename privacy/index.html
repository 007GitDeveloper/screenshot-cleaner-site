Screenshot Cleaner

Privacy policy

Last updated 15 November 2026

In short: your photos never leave your iPhone.

Screenshot Cleaner does all of its work on your phone. We don't have accounts, we don't upload your photos or their contents, and we don't use advertising or tracking.

What the app does with your photos

To find and sort your screenshots, Screenshot Cleaner needs access to your photo library. With your permission, it:

  • finds your screenshots and measures how much space they use;
  • reads the text in each screenshot and recognises what's in the picture, so it can sort them (for example tickets, receipts or sensitive information) and let you search them;
  • finds duplicate and similar photos, and large videos you might want to compress.

All of this is done on your iPhone, using Apple's built-in on-device technology (such as Vision, Core ML and, on supported iPhones, Apple Intelligence's on-device model) and an image-recognition model built into the app. The results (for example the text read from a screenshot) are saved only in the app's own storage on your phone so they don't have to be worked out again. They are never sent to us or anyone else, and they're removed when you delete the app.

Nothing is deleted without your confirmation. iOS asks you to approve every deletion, and deleted items go to Photos' "Recently Deleted" album, where your iPhone keeps them for 30 days.

Purchases

Screenshot Cleaner can be used for free up to a limit; a Pro subscription removes the limit. Payments are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

To check whether you have an active subscription, the app uses RevenueCat, a subscription service. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous identifier created by the app, your purchase and subscription history from the App Store, and basic technical information needed to process it (such as app version, device type and the App Store country). It does not receive your name, email address, photos or anything from your photo library. This information is used only to unlock Pro and restore your purchases.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, and no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • We don't sell or share personal information.

Your choices

  • You can change or remove the app's access to your photos at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos.
  • Deleting the app removes everything it saved on your phone.
  • You can manage or cancel your subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
  • If you'd like RevenueCat's subscription records about your anonymous identifier deleted, contact us and we'll arrange it.

Children

Screenshot Cleaner isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
