Common questions

I deleted screenshots but my storage hasn't gone down

iPhone keeps deleted photos in the Recently Deleted album for 30 days. To free the space now, open Photos › Albums › Recently Deleted, tap Select, then Delete All.

The app can only see some of my photos

You may have chosen "Limited Access". Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Screenshot Cleaner and choose Full Access.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything is done on your iPhone. See the privacy policy for details.

Sorting or search got something wrong

Sorting and search are done automatically on your phone, so they won't be perfect. The app always shows you what will be deleted before anything happens. If something is consistently wrong, let us know (without sending sensitive screenshots) and we'll improve it.

How do I restore or cancel my subscription?

To restore Pro on a new phone, open the app's Settings (the gear on the Screenshots tab) and tap Restore purchases. To cancel, go to iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

What happens to my original video when I compress it?

You choose. The app can save the full-quality original to Files first, keep both, or replace the original with the smaller copy. A replaced original stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days.
